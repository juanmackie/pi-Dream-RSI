## Cross-source design invariant

The optimizer may choose research directions, candidate edits, and policy proposals. It cannot author or access hidden answers, alter the evaluator, certify its own work, or apply a candidate directly. A separate admission and confirmation path decides whether a proposal is measurable, budget-feasible, oracle-clean, reproducible in a fresh process, and safe to apply. Every transition and cost is recorded append-only.

## Acceptance claims to keep separate

1. **Implemented** means the mechanism exists in code and is reachable through the repo-level interface.
2. **Verified locally** means a reproducible local command exercised it against this repository or named fixtures.
3. **Externally demonstrated** means the preregistered tournament ran on genuinely unseen repositories with trusted hidden benchmarks, budget-matched arms, external judging, and auditable results.

Passing the first two does not imply the third. The third also requires access to independent hidden benchmarks and each arm's executable baseline; absent those artifacts, report the tournament as blocked/inconclusive rather than fabricate a pass.
