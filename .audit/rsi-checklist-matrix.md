## Governing boundaries

- Preserve verified measurement, correctness gates, adversarial review, independent post-apply verification, trusted-local consent, scope checks, durable accounting, and fail-closed recovery.
- Keep commits local to the dedicated `dream-rsi/improve` branch. No push, PR creation, default-branch commit/merge, OS scheduler registration, deployment, or paid provider use without separate authorization.
- The user approved nightly caps of 6 campaigns, 400,000 tokens, and 240 wall minutes, with lifetime accounting preserved. This does not authorize unlimited spend. No provider/model configuration or provider-enforced per-call token bound was supplied; no real-model campaign may run without it.
- External repository proof and adoption metrics were explicitly skipped. Do not claim arbitrary-repository effectiveness, magnitude-level gains, or full evidence of adoption.
- Reject `git reset --hard` rollback. Preserve history and unrelated work; use verified revert/compensation with evidence.
