# Research organization policy

Find the largest bottleneck supported by measurements. State the metric, baseline,
budget, and correctness constraints before proposing a change. Work on one bounded
hypothesis at a time, keep independent directions alive when evidence is uncertain,
and record what changed, what the measurement showed, and what it cost. Treat prior
success as a search hint, never as proof that a new change works. Only the host's
independent evaluator can confirm an improvement or authorize a promotion.
